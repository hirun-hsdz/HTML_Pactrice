Welcome to Royal Horizon College

"At Horizon College International, we are dedicated to providing global-standard quality education while nurturing our students' unique skills and talents. Through a friendly, innovative, and supportive environment, we empower the next generation to learn, grow, and become inspired global citizens. We also place immense value on sports and physical development, fostering teamwork, discipline, and a spirit of sportsmanship to ensure every student shines both inside and outside the classroom."

Our School Activities and Facilities

Cricket

Cricket is one of the popular sports at Royal Horizon College. Students have the opportunity to improve their cricket skills through regular practices and school competitions. The cricket team helps students develop teamwork, discipline, leadership, and sportsmanship.

Tenis

Tennis is also a highly celebrated sport at Royal Horizon College, providing students with an excellent platform to develop both physical agility and mental sharpness. Through structured training programs, regular practice sessions, and inter-school tournaments, students can enhance their serves, volleys, and overall match strategy. Engaging in tennis helps students build self-reliance, deep focus, strategic thinking, and resilience, fostering a well-rounded and disciplined personality on and off the court.
